Tata Steel in the Dock, Students Walk Out, and a Road Sign That Says "Out" Twice

Tata Steel's criminal trial over air pollution opened in Amsterdam. Prosecutors allege 598 deliberate toxic releases and say they secretly hacked the company to gather evidence. We also cover students walking out over plans for a new military service, the grid congestion slowing house building, and the death of youth-care campaigner Jason Bhugwandass. Then a fire in the port, more fallout from the neo-Nazi documentary, an IDFA win for an Amsterdam filmmaker, Friesland's new bilingual road signs, and ideas for tonight in Amsterdam.

  • Tata Steel goes on trial: Prosecutors accuse Tata Steel IJmuiden of five offences, including producing toxic ‘raw’ coke 598 times without a permit. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl, frissewind.nu, tatasteelnederland.com)
  • Students walk out over military service: Several thousand students in sixteen cities left class to protest plans for a new selective military service. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl, Rijksoverheid, schoolbank.nl)
  • More homes finished, far fewer permits: CBS counted 18,100 new homes last quarter, but permits fell to 16,600 after a rush ahead of grid-congestion rules. (DutchNews.nl, NU.nl, CBS, binnenlandsbestuur.nl)
  • Youth-care campaigner Jason Bhugwandass dies at 28: He spent a decade fighting locked youth care after passing through five institutions as a teenager. (AT5, NU.nl, CBS, binnenlandsbestuur.nl)
  • Fire on a scrap-metal ship in the port: Firefighters used fireboats and divers to fight a major blaze in the hold of the SSI PHOENIX at Westpoort. (NH Nieuws, AT5, noord-holland.headliner.nl, drimble.nl)
  • Neo-Nazi documentary fallout: dentist suspended: Dentists’ association KNMT suspended Bas Eybergen after a neo-Nazi cage-fighting event was filmed on his property. (NOS, NU.nl, fvd.nl, anarchistischefederatie.nl)
  • Amsterdam filmmaker wins IDFA’s top prize: Louis Hothothot’s ‘The Year of the Ghost’ won three awards, including best film in the International Competition. (DutchNews.nl, AT5, festival.idfa.nl, filmvandaag.nl)
  • Friesland’s bilingual road signs: The province has put up its first Dutch-Frisian exit sign on the N356, with 33 planned in the first batch. (NU.nl, omropfryslan.nl, tpo.nl)
