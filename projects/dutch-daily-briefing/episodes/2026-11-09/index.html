Red Line March, Youth Care Bills and a Wegenwacht Strike

Organisers say at least 200,000 people marched in Amsterdam's Red Line protest against Dutch policy on Israel. There were four arrests, and the prime minister stayed away. We also cover towns raising taxes to pay for youth care, today's Wegenwacht strike, a rebellion inside the VVD, leaked Russian death records, a record day of Eredivisie goals and a painting coming home to the Amsterdam Museum.
