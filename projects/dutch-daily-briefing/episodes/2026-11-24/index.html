Alarm at Erasmus MC's Intensive Care, and the Wolf Cull Plan

The health inspectorate says the work culture at Erasmus MC's intensive care unit could seriously threaten patient safety, and Covid-era figure Diederik Gommers has stepped down as its head. We also cover the cabinet's plan to allow wolf culling, a Dutch delegation heading to India over Insiya, a new request to do your laundry outside peak hours, and a longer stay for the asylum centre in Osdorp. Plus ABN AMRO returning to the Ajax shirt, a new line-up for De Toppers, tonight's supermoon and what's on in Amsterdam.

  • Inspectorate intervenes at Erasmus MC intensive care: The health inspectorate says staff on Erasmus MC’s adult ICU didn’t feel safe raising concerns, which it says puts patient safety at risk. (NL Times, NOS, erasmusmc.nl)
  • Cabinet moves to allow wolf culling: Provinces could get permits to shoot wolves without problem behaviour, with shooting possible from next summer at the earliest. (NOS, NU.nl, welingelichtekringen.nl, ermelosweekblad.nl)
  • Delegation heads to India over Insiya: Dutch officials are pressing India to return Insiya, taken by her father in 2016, and to extradite him. (DutchNews.nl, NOS, Hart van Nederland, indiankanoon.org)
  • Do your laundry outside peak hours, government asks: A ministry campaign asks households to run washers, dryers and EV charging outside 4 to 9 p.m. to ease grid pressure. (NU.nl, Rijksoverheid, bright.nl)
  • Osdorp asylum centre set to stay open much longer: Amsterdam wants the Willinklaan centre open at least ten more years, cutting residents from about 700 to 650. (AT5, Het Parool, Reformatorisch Dagblad, NH Nieuws)
  • ABN AMRO set to return to the Ajax shirt: Ajax is close to a reported five-year, €60 million main sponsorship deal with ABN AMRO starting next season. (AT5, NU.nl, NH Nieuws, adformatie.nl)
  • René Froger leaves De Toppers: Froger quits after the 2027 ArenA concerts, and Mart Hoogkamer and Tino Martin will replace him and Gerard Joling. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Supermoon over a cold, clear evening: Tuesday’s full Beaver Moon should be visible under mostly clear skies, with some areas possibly staying below freezing all day later this week. (NL Times)
