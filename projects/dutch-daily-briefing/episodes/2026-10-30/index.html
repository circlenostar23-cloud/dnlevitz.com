The Right's Price List, Shell's Windfall, and a Panda Says Goodbye

Three right-wing opposition parties have teamed up and set their price for backing the minority cabinet's tax plan. Their demands cover tax, asylum, nitrogen and abortion. We also cover Shell's doubled profit and your energy bill, the ECB's pause and Christine Lagarde's surprise early exit, and a push to ban AI in primary schools. Plus a panda's farewell, a World Series title with Dutch roots and a rock star's painting that hung at the tax office.

  • A right-wing bloc sets its price for the tax plan: JA21, BBB and SGP will back the tax plan as one bloc if the cabinet drops its NATO defence tax rise. (DutchNews.nl, NU.nl)
  • Shell’s profit doubles as energy bills climb: Shell’s adjusted third-quarter profit hit 10.8 billion dollars, mostly because Middle East refinery attacks pushed fuel prices up. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • ECB holds rates, and Lagarde is leaving early: The ECB kept its deposit rate at 2.5 percent after two hikes this year, with eurozone inflation at 3.8 percent. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • MPs want generative AI out of primary schools: A Volt and CDA motion to ban tools like ChatGPT for primary pupils has backing from parties holding 88 of 150 seats. (NL Times, NOS)
  • Seven officers to stand trial over Rotterdam arrest death: Prosecutors have dropped one of eight cases, against a trainee, over a 32-year-old man’s death during a 2023 arrest. (NL Times, NU.nl)
  • Courier tasered on Amsterdam’s Runstraat: Police tasered a courier who wouldn’t move his van, and a bystander’s video shows him being hit at close range. (Het Parool, NOS)
  • Lang Yue the panda heads to China: Ouwehands Dierenpark’s second cub, born in 2024, is joining China’s breeding programme under the zoo’s agreement before she turns four. (NOS)
  • A former Dutch coach wins the World Series: Pat Murphy, who coached the Dutch national team twice, managed Milwaukee to its first title, beating Tampa Bay 4-1. (NL Times, NOS)
  • Herman Brood’s tax-office painting joins the national collection: An acrylic of two men with a pistol, checked for forgery for months, is the first Brood work the state owns. (NOS, NU.nl)
