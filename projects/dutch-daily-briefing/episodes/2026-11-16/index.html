Day Four at the Catshuis

The coalition crisis talks move to the Catshuis in The Hague as business leaders and mayors warn against letting the cabinet fall. We also look at deliberate GPS jamming over Schiphol, hacked ads on popular Dutch websites that targeted older iPhones, the wave of rental homes being sold off, the first New Year's Eve under the fireworks ban, and German opposition to Dutch nuclear plans. Plus a disputed Van Gogh, a police boat to the rescue for Sinterklaas, and what's on in Amsterdam tonight.
