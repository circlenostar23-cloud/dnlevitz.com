France Counts the Cost, and a Lost Sunday at Schiphol

A ten-hour failure of Schiphol's passport e-gates left several hundred travellers watching their flights leave without them, and most can't claim compensation. France counts almost 2,400 arrests after a day of riots, and President Macron's TV address draws heavy criticism. Also today: Albert Heijn's app comes back, Dutch families take refugees into their homes, Amsterdam's culture scene pushes back on the tourist tax, and two Dutch athletes have very different weekends.
