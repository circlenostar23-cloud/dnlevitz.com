Zelensky in The Hague, and Booking.com Wins on Office Days

Zelensky visits The Hague and the Netherlands pledges billions for Ukraine, though the money had already been set aside. Also today: a deadly train crash in Spain, a court backs Booking.com on office days, pension funds flush with cash, bird flu keeps poultry indoors, gas breaks €100 and the Vondelpark fatbike ban stays. Plus a royal lunch, a Roman glass trick recreated, and what's on in Amsterdam this weekend.

Thursday, 29 October 2026 · 15 min

Today's headlines

0:0014:34

  • Zelensky in The Hague: After meeting Zelensky, Prime Minister Jetten announced a €500 million winter package for Ukrainian air defence and drones. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Deadly train crash in Spain: A Renfe train hit a stationary Iryo train south of Madrid, killing at least 68 people and injuring over 179. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Booking.com can require office days: An Amsterdam court ruled Booking.com may require staff to work 40 percent of their time in the office. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl)
  • Pension funds flush ahead of the switch: ABP and PME now have funding ratios above 132 percent, mainly because interest rates have risen sharply. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Bird flu: poultry ordered indoors: After outbreaks at three farms in three days, nearly 96,000 birds were culled and all Dutch poultry must stay indoors. (NL Times, NOS)
  • Gas price breaks 100 euros: Europe’s benchmark TTF gas price passed €100 per megawatt-hour, its highest since December 2022, hitting households on dynamic contracts first. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Vondelpark fatbike ban stays: Amsterdam is keeping its fatbike ban in the Vondelpark, which cut fatbike traffic there by over 85 percent, but won’t extend it. (NL Times, NU.nl)
  • Lunch at the palace: The King and Queen hosted 27 high achievers at Noordeinde Palace, including Suzan & Freek and writer Connie Palmen. (NOS)
  • The Roman glass trick: Sheffield researchers say they’ve recreated the Lycurgus Cup’s green-to-red colour change using gold and silver nanoparticles in glass. (NU.nl)
