Ambushed: A Tax Deal Puts Jetten's Coalition on the Brink

D66 and the CDA made a deal on a millionaire's tax with the left-wing opposition, and the VVD, their own coalition partner, says it was ambushed. Also today: a top court ruling on Syrian asylum claims, unemployment rising faster among migrants, the government dropping its goal of ending homelessness, Uber Eats winning in court and a cooler housing market. Then the carnival season opens in the south, IDFA opens in Amsterdam and a young Cruyff gets called up for the US.

  • Tax deal pushes the coalition to the brink: D66 and CDA sided with Pro and Volt, without the VVD, to shift about €1 billion a year in tax from work to wealth. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl)
  • Court orders a new look at whether Syria is safe: The Council of State told Asylum Minister Van den Brink his Syria policy isn’t adequately justified after a Syrian torture survivor appealed. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Unemployment rising faster among migrants: CBS says first-generation immigrant joblessness hit 6.9%, while it barely moved for people of Dutch origin, at 3%. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Government drops goal of ending homelessness by 2030: Minister Mirjam Sterk says zero homelessness is unachievable, drawing criticism from MPs; about 33,000 people have no fixed home. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Court blocks Uber Eats delivery ban in Amsterdam: The Council of State halted the Labour Inspectorate’s one-month ban over couriers without work permits, citing unconsidered consequences. (Het Parool)
  • Housing market confidence slides: A TU Delft monitor says overbidding is over, as mortgage rates hit 3.93% and borrowing capacity fell by up to 11%. (NU.nl)
  • Carnival season opens on the 11th of the 11th: Crowds in Noord-Brabant and Limburg braved rain and cold at 11:11 a.m., with Tilburg tightening rules on drunk minors. (NL Times, NOS)
  • IDFA documentary festival opens: The 39th edition shows 253 films from 80 countries across Amsterdam until 22 November, with tickets at €13.50. (DutchNews.nl)
  • A Cruyff in an American shirt: Johan Cruyff’s 17-year-old grandson Shae Cruijff-Beane has been called up to the US under-18 squad to face Norway and Portugal. (DutchNews.nl)
