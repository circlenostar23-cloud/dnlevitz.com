€18 Billion to Fix Bridges, and the Lelylijn Is Shelved

The cabinet is putting €18 billion into the country's ageing bridges, locks and roads, and shelving 29 projects to pay for it, including the Lelylijn and part of Amsterdam's Zuidasdok. Also today: a suspected arson in Nieuw-West, a Dutch bank caught up in US sanctions on the International Criminal Court, farms making way for F-35s, rising energy bills and Albert Heijn's longer delivery outage. Plus Netflix's AI dubbing flop, Floor Jansen leaving Nightwish, and ideas for tonight in Amsterdam.

Read more

  • €18 billion for ageing infrastructure, and the Lelylijn shelved: The cabinet’s putting €18 billion through 2041 into 175 worn-out roads, bridges and locks, with 29 planned projects paused. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Suspected arson in Nieuw-West: A garage fire in Amsterdam forced 55 homes to evacuate and left the building unlivable; a 26-year-old man’s been arrested. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl)
  • US sanctions on the ICC put ABN Amro in a bind: ABN Amro, which holds the court’s account, risks US fines or sanctions once the measures take full effect in April 2027. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Farms make way for F-35s at Lelystad Airport: Defence now needs 240 hectares around Lelystad Airport, so four farms and six businesses will eventually have to leave. (NL Times, NOS)
  • Energy bills rise on variable contracts: About 3.7 million households saw bills rise around €25 a month on 1 October as gas prices climbed. (NU.nl)
  • Albert Heijn’s delivery outage drags on: After a cyberattack, Albert Heijn won’t deliver online groceries through Tuesday, and customers still can’t place new orders. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • TU Delft’s quota for women blocked: The education minister has refused TU Delft’s plan to reserve 30 percent of Aerospace Engineering places for women. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • AI backlash: a dubbing flop and an essay prize scandal: Netflix pulled Spike after its AI Dutch dubbing drew criticism, and two Zwagerman Prize essays were withdrawn over suspected AI use. (NU.nl)
  • Floor Jansen leaves Nightwish: After 13 years, singer Floor Jansen’s leaving Nightwish by mutual agreement to focus on solo work, including a January 2027 album. (NOS)
