A 'Culture of Fear' at Picnic-PostNL

Seventy-one riders and staff describe a years-long culture of fear at the Dutch cycling team Picnic-PostNL, and the team promises an independent inquiry. We also cover the first Dutch-French talks on nuclear weapons, an EU ruling that could change how banks treat scam victims, and data centres clogging the queue for the power grid. Plus an update on Albert Heijn, Ajax's rough night in Split, a looted Kandinsky from the Dam, the end of the leap second, and ideas for your weekend in Amsterdam.

Friday, 16 October 2026 · 15 min

  • A ‘culture of fear’ at Picnic-PostNL: NOS and In De Waaier spoke with 71 current and former staff, who describe punished criticism and ignored mental-health problems. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Dutch-French talks on nuclear weapons: Dutch and French officials held the first Nuclear Steering Group meeting in Paris, though nothing agreed has been announced. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • EU court: banks must refund fraud victims first: Banks must refund unauthorised payments by the next working day, even if they think the customer was grossly negligent. (NU.nl)
  • Data centres clog the grid queue: Data centres want about 15 gigawatts, nearly a third of the queue, and grid operators want the cabinet to set limits. (NOS)
  • Albert Heijn: shelves restocked, deliveries still off: Stores are getting deliveries again, but home delivery is off until at least Sunday and the website’s still down. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Arrests in Split as Ajax loses: Croatian police arrested 57 people overnight to stop about 500 Ajax and Hajduk fans fighting in central Split. (NOS, Het Parool)
  • A looted Kandinsky from the Dam sells for millions: Kandinsky’s Das bunte Leben, once owned by a Jewish Amsterdam couple, fetched about £31.6 million at Christie’s London. (DutchNews.nl, NL Times)
  • Goodbye, leap second: Measurement authorities meeting in Versailles voted to scrap the extra second, which has disrupted computer networks since 1972. (NOS, NU.nl)

(Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 October; festival 10:00–21:00, picking your own fruit 10:00–17:00, Landgoed de Olmenhorst, Lisserbroek (just outside Amsterdam))
