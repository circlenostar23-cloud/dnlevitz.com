Climate Summit Collapses, a Patchy Bus Strike, and a Message for Insiya

The UN climate summit in Türkiye has ended without a final deal for the first time since 2009, after the EU rejected a text that left out fossil fuels. We also cover the regional bus and train strike, a runaway escalator at Bijlmer ArenA, the first frost of winter, and Amsterdam mayor Femke Halsema's attempt to get a message past an Indian internet block to Insiya, who was abducted from the city ten years ago. Plus Verstappen's win in Las Vegas, a hard year for the Groot Dictee and what's on in Amsterdam tonight.
