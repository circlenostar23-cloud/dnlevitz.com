Storm Boelo Turns Deadly, and Serbia's Students Win

Storm Boelo, the season's first official storm, killed a cyclist in Amsterdam, left a kitesurfer missing off The Hague and grounded dozens of flights at Schiphol. We also cover doctors pushing back on the health minister's long covid plans, a court ruling that matters to international families, and Serbs queueing in The Hague as Vucic concedes defeat. Plus Tallon Griekspoor's fiery title win and Queen Máxima on the dance floor.
