Climate Targets on the Clock, Cash in the Drawer

An appeal court says the Dutch state must keep writing binding climate targets while it appeals the Bonaire climate case. The central bank warns about AI-powered cyberattacks and a tech-stock crash, and the first New Year's Eve under the fireworks ban is getting closer. Plus: train fares, a parliamentary fight over the word "remigration", a billion-euro housing deal, Steve McQueen's Erasmus Prize and a skating record set before the ice melted.

  • Bonaire climate case: the state must keep working on binding targets: An appeals court mostly refused to pause a ruling ordering emissions targets by July 2027 and an adaptation plan covering Bonaire. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl, dossierkoninkrijksrelaties.nl, tpo.nl)
  • Central bank warns of AI cyberattacks and a tech crash: DNB says AI is making cyberattacks faster and stronger, and a successful attack could one day knock out payments. (NL Times, NOS, DNB, CNBC)
  • First New Year’s Eve under the fireworks ban: Police will use drones, cameras and number-plate recognition at the Belgian and German borders to stop illegal fireworks coming in. (NL Times, NU.nl, welingelichtekringen.nl, Omroep West)
  • Update: train fares frozen until at least May: NS’s planned 4.7 percent rise is on hold, paid for with €47 million left over from last summer’s discount-ticket fund. (NOS, Reformatorisch Dagblad, treinenweb.nl)
  • Parliament wants ‘remigration’ out of a Dutch law: A Tweede Kamer majority wants the 1999 law on voluntary return support renamed and rewritten in neutral language. (NOS, NL Times, parlement.com)
  • Rabobank buys nearly 3,000 rental homes from Vesteda: Rabobank’s paying €983 million for 2,828 mid-rent homes, and tenants’ rents and contracts won’t change. (NL Times, mena.nl, NOS)
  • Steve McQueen receives the Erasmus Prize: King Willem-Alexander gave the British director the €150,000 award at Amsterdam’s Royal Palace, where he got a standing ovation. (NL Times, erasmusprijs.org, depont.nl)
  • Natural ice, and a one-hour world record: Judith Krabbenborg skated 34.878 km in an hour in Winterswijk, setting a women’s world record on natural ice. (DutchNews.nl, NOS, en.wikipedia.org)
