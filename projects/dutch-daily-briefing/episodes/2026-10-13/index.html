Albert Heijn's Cyber Outage, the End of the 13-Week Scan, and U.S. Sanctions on the ICC

A cybersecurity incident at Ahold Delhaize has stopped several Albert Heijn distribution centres and knocked its apps offline, and fresh-food shelves may empty. We also cover the health minister scrapping the 13-week pregnancy scan, the Dutch response to U.S. sanctions on the International Criminal Court, the tax fight over the "freedom contribution", and the row over Israeli fans at AZ Alkmaar. Then the seal centre in Pieterburen fighting bankruptcy, the cabaret prizes, a panda heading to China and what's on in Amsterdam tonight.

Read more

  • Albert Heijn hit by cybersecurity incident: Ahold Delhaize shut down systems, taking Albert Heijn, Etos and Gall & Gall websites, apps and deliveries offline. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl)
  • The 13-week pregnancy scan is being scrapped: From 2028 Dutch women won’t get the extra scan, after the Health Council found it missed 1,164 serious abnormalities. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • U.S. sanctions on the International Criminal Court: PM Rob Jetten vowed to defend the Hague court ‘brick by brick’ after Washington blacklisted it, with a licence running until April 2027. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl)
  • Warehouse fire in Aalsmeer closes a Schiphol runway: Smoke from a burning car storage hall briefly shut the Buitenveldertbaan, and five nearby homes were evacuated; no one was hurt. (NL Times, Het Parool)
  • Opposition rejects the ‘freedom contribution’ tax: Left and right call the defence-funding plan a sneaky tax rise, worth about 5 billion euros a year from households and employers. (NU.nl)
  • Israel presses Alkmaar to let in fans for the AZ match: Israel’s ambassador urged Mayor Anja Schouten to admit Israeli fans or move the Hapoel Be’er Sheva match, but the ban stands. (NOS)
  • The Pieterburen seal centre faces bankruptcy: With a €2.8 million deficit and no help from the municipality or province, the Lauwersoog centre expects bankruptcy within two weeks. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • The Poelifinario cabaret awards: Ruud Smulders, Lisa Ostermann and Yentl en de Boer won the top Dutch cabaret prizes at a gala honouring Paulien Cornelisse. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Panda cub Lang Yue is moving to China: Ouwehands Dierenpark’s two-year-old panda leaves at the end of the month to join a Chinese breeding programme. (NOS, NU.nl)

Also in the news

  • Lavreysen’s main rival Richardson sent home before the track cycling worlds over alleged racism: British Cycling dropped Matthew Richardson in Shanghai as The Times reports an inquiry into alleged discriminatory remarks. (NU.nl, NOS)
  • Grid congestion is holding back factories’ efforts to go green: Glass, cardboard and brick makers can’t switch to electric machines because the grid lacks capacity, a Cluster6 report says. (NL Times, NOS)
  • Flu vaccination rate hits a new low: Only just over half of eligible Dutch people get a flu shot, while flu kills about 4,700 a year. (NL Times)
