A Tax Deal Without a Majority, Hacked Freezers, and the 34-Cent Stroopwafel

The coalition has agreed a revised tax plan and a one-year delay to the new wealth tax, but it heads into Wednesday's debate without a majority, a day after the Council of State tore into the timetable. We also cover hackers turning shop freezers into heaters, a grid connection freeze in Gelderland, a record €2 million reward for 'Bolle Jos', and a stroopwafel price war. Plus what's on in Amsterdam tonight.

Tuesday, 3 November 2026 · 14 min

Today's headlines

0:0014:14

Read more

  • Coalition agrees tax plan, delays wealth tax, still no majority: D66, VVD and CDA push the new box 3 capital gains tax back to 2029 and offer both opposition blocs some tax relief. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Hackers turn up the heat on shop freezers: Hackers remotely raised fridge and freezer temperatures at 17 or more Dutch businesses and dozens of Belgian stores, Danfoss says. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Gelderland grid freeze: Sixteen municipalities in the Achterhoek, Liemers and Stedendriehoek can’t get new or heavier connections, backdated to applications since 1 July. (NU.nl, NOS)
  • 74 municipalities now under pressure over asylum housing: Asylum Minister Bart van den Brink put 11 more municipalities, including Castricum, under active supervision for housing too few asylum seekers. (NL Times)
  • Record reward for ‘Bolle Jos’: Prosecutors now offer €2 million for tips on Jos Leijdekkers, a drug trafficker believed to be in Sierra Leone. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Hotel fire in Amsterdam-Zuidoost: About 140 guests were evacuated overnight from a Johan Cruijff Boulevard hotel and four people inhaled smoke. (NOS, Het Parool)
  • KNMI switches to local weather warnings: Weather warnings now cover areas meteorologists draw on a map rather than whole provinces. (NL Times, NOS)
  • The Gashouder reopens: After an 18-month renovation, the Westergas venue has a new basement for facilities and acoustic panels that remove its famous echo. (Het Parool)
  • The stroopwafel price war: Dirk now sells 12 stroopwafels for 34 cents, and a retail expert says supermarkets earn nothing on them. (DutchNews.nl)
