Fix It First: 29 Projects Shelved, and the Prime Minister's Baby News

The cabinet is putting 29 road, rail and waterway projects on hold, including part of Amsterdam's Zuidasdok, to pay for overdue repairs. Rob Jetten's baby news puts the surrogacy bill back in the spotlight. Plus Thursday's regional transport strike, curfews in France, Albert Heijn's police report, more unrest in Floradorp, remembering Coos Huijsen, Sanne Wevers' farewell, and a very wet promotion for Princess Amalia.
