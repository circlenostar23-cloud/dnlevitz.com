Dutch Spies, Kushner's Phone and a Second Storm

A new book claims Dutch military intelligence hacked Jared Kushner's iPhone and watched Gulf money move through an account he controlled. Kushner denies it and the MIVD won't comment. Also today: prosecutors' new timeline in the killing of Hajar, Storm Austen and tonight's Storm Boelo, the PHP flaw now being exploited, Amsterdam weighing a ban on takeaway packaging, Michael van Gerwen falling out of the darts top ten, and a €3 million rescue for Sinterklaas's steamboat.
