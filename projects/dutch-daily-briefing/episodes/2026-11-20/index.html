A Drone at the Dutch Embassy, and Hard Questions for Destinus

A Russian drone came down behind the Dutch embassy in Kyiv on the same day NRC reported that staff of the Dutch arms maker Destinus had operated drones in the war. We also cover the UN court's first-ever genocide ruling against a state, the money squeeze at housing corporations, the vote to block next year's train fare rise, arrests over planned attacks on Revolut customers, and FVD's expulsions. Plus a new director for the Stedelijk, the Dance Parade's return to Rotterdam, a Dutch record at the darts, and what's on in Amsterdam this weekend.

  • A new director for the Stedelijk: Krist Gruijthuijsen, who now runs Finland’s Espoo Museum of Modern Art, will succeed Rein Wolfs on 10 July next year. (AT5, NU.nl, artreview.com, theartnewspaper.com)
  • Rotterdam’s Dance Parade returns: The free DJ truck parade comes back on 18 September 2027, 18 years after it stopped following the 2009 beach riots. (NOS, NU.nl, festivalfans.nl, Rijnmond)
