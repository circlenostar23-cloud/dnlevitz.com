Amsterdam's Poor Fall Further Behind

New CBS figures show Amsterdam has the highest poverty rate of any big Dutch city, and freelancers are a growing share of the poor. Also today: Brussels warns the Netherlands is spending too fast, Covid in sewage hits a post-pandemic high, Gasunie calls for a strategic gas reserve, and an Amsterdam alderman makes the case for road tolls. Plus complaints about the budget train to Berlin, Tidal's purge of AI music, a street buried in starling droppings, and what's on in Amsterdam this weekend.

  • Amsterdam tops the poverty table: CBS says 6 percent of Amsterdammers lived below the poverty line in 2025, double the national rate. (DutchNews.nl, AT5, CBS)
  • Brussels: Dutch spending growing too fast: The European Commission says Dutch net spending will exceed agreed EU limits in 2026 and 2027. (NL Times, edgeconsultancykw.com, economy-finance.ec.europa.eu)
  • Covid in sewage at post-pandemic high: RIVM finds seven times more virus in wastewater than mid-October, though illness appears mostly mild. (NU.nl, transport-online.nl, nieuws.nl)
  • Gasunie wants a strategic gas reserve: With storage at a historically low 59 percent, state operator Gasunie warns supply could quickly tighten this winter. (NU.nl, nieuws.nl, binnenland.headliner.nl)
  • Amsterdam alderman makes the case for tolls: D66’s Elise Moeskops wants local authorities able to charge tolls to fund new roads and public transport. (AT5, topgear.nl)
  • Complaints pile up over the budget train to Berlin: Passengers’ group Rover reports cancellations, skipped stops and unpaid claims on GoVolta’s Amsterdam–Berlin service. (Het Parool, transport-online.nl, spoorpro.nl)
  • Tidal bans AI music, BumaStemra goes to court: Tidal will remove fully AI-made tracks and reject new ones from 9 December, after AI made 43 percent of recent submissions. (NOS, NU.nl, ibtimes.co.uk, nieuws.nl)
  • Starlings take over a street in West: Roosting starlings have coated bikes and cars on Spaarndammerbuurt’s Aert van Nesstraat in smelly droppings. (AT5)
