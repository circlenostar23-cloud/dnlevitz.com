A Box 3 Majority, an Uzi Deal and a Village Below the Sea

A House majority is forming behind the cabinet's rushed overhaul of the savings and investment tax, and Prime Minister Jetten is skipping the UN climate summit to secure it. We also cover a Tilburg arms maker building Israeli-designed rifles, a new village planned at the country's lowest point, Amsterdam's tourist tax fight and a tense Kristallnacht commemoration. Then: cheaper groceries, the André Hazes music rights sale, a royal trip to Qatar and the Sinterklaas who still hasn't shown up.

  • Box 3 tax overhaul nears a majority; Jetten skips COP31: JA21 backs taxing box 3 capital gains only when shares or property are sold, giving the coalition a House majority. (NL Times, NOS)
  • Tilburg firm to build Israeli-designed rifles: AIA will make IWI’s Arad 5 rifle and Negev machine gun for NATO and European buyers, at an undisclosed price. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl)
  • Five new fast-track housing sites, and a village 6.78 metres below sea level: Assen, Best, Roosendaal, Walcheren and Zuidplas will get state help to build about 25,000 homes by 2040. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Amsterdam’s tourist tax fight: Forty-seven cultural and hospitality leaders want the city to pause raising its overnight tax from 12.5 to 20 percent by 2031. (NL Times, Het Parool)
  • Kristallnacht commemoration turns political: A Jewish leader thanked Jetten for resisting demands from Sunday’s Red Line march, which organisers say drew over 200,000. (NOS)
  • Groceries cheaper than a year ago: Good harvests pushed food prices down, with butter about 31 percent cheaper in September, the central bank says. (NU.nl)
  • Rachel Hazes sells André Hazes’s music rights: His widow sold the catalogue to John Ewbank’s Pythagoras Music Fund, so it won’t pass to her children. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • King and queen to visit Qatar: From 9 to 11 December, it’s the first Dutch state visit to the Middle East in fourteen years. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Still no new Sinterklaas: The Sinterklaasjournaal’s first episode found the saint’s study empty, so he’ll only be revealed Saturday in Grave. (NOS, NU.nl)
