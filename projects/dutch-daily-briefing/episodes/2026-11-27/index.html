Knot Loses the ECB, and the EU Budget Talks Turn Icy

Dutch central banker Klaas Knot loses the race to lead the European Central Bank, and EU leaders leave Brussels without a budget deal, with the Dutch rebate at the center of the fight. Plus: more than 600 Amsterdam taxis frozen in a money-laundering probe, a plan to close the ER in Amsterdam-Oost, a Dutch pushback on a US bill targeting ASML, and Meta's camera glasses in court. We end with the coldest November in decades, the Light Festival and your weekend in Amsterdam.

  • Knot loses the ECB race as EU budget talks turn icy: Spain’s Pablo Hernández de Cos, 55, will lead the ECB from February 7 after a French-German deal on top jobs. (NL Times, NOS, rte.ie, en.ara.cat)
  • Police freeze more than 600 Amsterdam taxis: Police seized cars from 26 linked companies in a money-laundering probe that also found forged driver cards and unlicensed taxis. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl, 112nederland.nl)
  • OLVG plans to close its ER in Amsterdam-Oost: From 2032, all of OLVG’s emergency care will move to OLVG West, though the Oost hospital stays open for planned care. (AT5, Het Parool, olvg.nl, amsterdamsehuisartsen.nl)
  • The Netherlands pushes back on a US bill targeting ASML: Trade minister Sjoerd Sjoerdsma says the MATCH Act, which would stop ASML servicing machines in China, goes “far, far too far.” (NL Times, DutchNews.nl, theepochtimes.com, voltgridnews.com)
  • Pressure grows on VVD leader Yeşilgöz: Party figures say members are deeply unhappy that she’s also defense minister and deputy prime minister. (DutchNews.nl, NU.nl, en.wikipedia.org, NL Times)
  • Groups ask a court to ban Meta’s camera glasses: The Consumentenbond and two foundations told an Amsterdam court the glasses make secret filming too easy because the recording light’s hard to see. (DutchNews.nl, NL Times, Rechtspraak, deccanchronicle.com)
  • Equal Pay Day: a delayed law and an underpaid judge: Dutch women earn 9.8% less per hour than men, and the pay transparency law won’t make its January 1, 2027 date either. (NOS, NU.nl, tpo.nl, vbk.nl)
  • Coldest November since 1998, and a marathon on natural ice: De Bilt is on track for 17 frost nights and an average of 4.3 degrees, three below normal. (NU.nl, NH Nieuws, maassluis.nu, weerplaza.nl)
  • Amsterdam Light Festival opens: The 15th edition has 19 light artworks on a new city-center route, and you can see them free from the street. (AT5, NH Nieuws, iamsterdam.com, amsterdamlightfestival.com)
