FVD Expels Two Over Neo-Nazi Links; Showdown Over Train Fares

Forum voor Democratie has expelled two members, one of them a Den Bosch council leader, after a Zembla investigation into neo-Nazi cage fights. MPs move to freeze next year's train fares, regional transport strikes go ahead on Monday and Tuesday, and Amsterdam asks for help treating crack addiction. Also: a record price for a Van Gogh, helmets for kids in e-cargo bikes, a home battery boom, a Dutch darts upset, and what's on in Amsterdam this weekend.

  • MPs move to freeze train fares: A Tweede Kamer majority backs a PvdD motion to stop NS’s 4.7 percent fare rise planned for 1 January 2027. (NL Times, NOS, Reformatorisch Dagblad, treinreiziger.nl)

  • Record price for Van Gogh after deal with Jewish heirs: After the owners settled with the Fischer heirs, ‘Châtaigniers en fleurs’ sold at Sotheby’s New York for $190.4 million. (DutchNews.nl, NL Times, yahoo.com, rte.ie)
  • Helmet rule covers kids in e-cargo bikes: Children riding as passengers on e-bikes and electric bakfietsen must wear helmets, though the parents pedalling them won’t. (NOS, Rijksoverheid, bright.nl)
  • Home battery boom as net metering ends: Dutch households with home batteries more than doubled this year to almost 430,000 as net metering ends on 1 January. (NOS, consumentenbond.nl, energievergelijker.nl)
  • Wattimena knocks Littler out of the Grand Slam of Darts: World number 19 Jermaine Wattimena beat two-time defending champion Luke Littler 10-8 in the round of 16, averaging 101.70. (NOS, NU.nl, sports.yahoo.com, skysports.com)
  • Women cleared over séance at children’s graves: A court acquitted four women who held a filmed paranormal session at children’s graves in Rusthof cemetery in 2021. (DutchNews.nl, NOS, RTV Utrecht)
