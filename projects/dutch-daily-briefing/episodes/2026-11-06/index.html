KLM's Very Bad Thursday: 5,000 Jobs Cut and a Wave of Bomb Hoaxes

KLM is cutting about 5,000 jobs and warns that its survival is "not a given". On the same day, one of its planes was caught up in a wave of hoax bomb threats against flights across Europe. Also today: cheaper petrol, more time for working refugees to finish civic integration, a metro hijacked by a graffiti crew, tickets for The Passion, Barney's 34th World Championship, a tiny fossil from Maastricht, and what's on in Amsterdam this weekend.

  • KLM cuts 5,000 jobs: CEO Marjan Rintel says almost 15 percent of staff will go, mostly through voluntary departures rather than compulsory layoffs. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Hoax bomb threats across Europe: Dozens of flights were hit, including a KLM Tel Aviv flight searched in Bucharest and later cancelled over a technical fault. (NL Times, NU.nl)
  • Petrol below €2.50, and live pump prices: Euro95 fell to €2.485 a litre on Thursday, its lowest since July, as gas and oil prices dropped. (NU.nl, NOS)
  • More time for working refugees: Refugees working at least 28 hours a week could get up to three extra years for civic integration. (NOS)
  • Update: migrant boat left from Dutch soil: Prosecutors confirm the boat carrying 36 Afghans, six of them minors, left Dutch territory, probably from Zeeuws-Vlaanderen. (NL Times, NU.nl)
  • Graffiti crew stops an Amsterdam metro: About twenty masked people threw a bike on the track and covered a line 51 train in graffiti with passengers trapped inside. (NU.nl, NOS)
  • The Passion starts charging for tickets: Next year’s edition in Ootmarsum on 25 March will cost €7.50, with limited tickets and priority for residents. (NU.nl, NOS)
  • Barney makes his 34th World Championship: Raymond van Barneveld secured his spot at Alexandra Palace with a 6-2 win over Jimmy van Schie in Leicester. (NU.nl, NOS)
  • A tiny, very rare fossil from Maastricht: Researchers described a 1.55-millimetre extinct crustacean from the former ENCI quarry that lived just before the dinosaurs died out. (NU.nl, NOS)
