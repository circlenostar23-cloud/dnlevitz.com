Out, In, Out Again: The Netherlands Skips Eurovision

A month after saying it would go back, the Dutch public broadcaster pulls the Netherlands out of Eurovision 2027 over Israel's participation. Israel's election ends with no clear winner. Also today: the watchdog blocks the sale of Unox, Schiphol's nature permit could stay in limbo until 2028, a new mortgage rule for homes with bad foundations, and an amateur club's big night in the Dutch cup.

Wednesday, 28 October 2026 · 14 min

  • The Netherlands drops out of Eurovision after all: Broadcaster NPO won’t send an act to Burgas in 2027, citing too little support amid objections to Israel’s participation. (DutchNews.nl, NOS)
  • Israel votes, and nobody wins outright: Exit polls show Netanyahu’s Likud losing five seats to 27, with neither bloc near the 61 needed for a majority. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Watchdog blocks the sale of Unox: Regulator ACM stopped Zwanenberg buying Unox and Zwan, saying it’d control 60 to 85 percent of rookworst and soup markets. (NL Times, NOS)
  • Schiphol’s nature permit could stay in limbo until 2028: The Council of State is weighing whether to refer nitrogen questions to the EU Court of Justice, which would pause the case. (NU.nl)
  • Bad foundations could mean a smaller mortgage: From 2027, NHG lenders must deduct expected foundation repair costs, averaging €92,000, from loans on homes rated D or E. (NU.nl, NOS)
  • A crypto recluse tops the Quote 500: Bitfinex chief and former Tether CEO Jean-Louis van der Velde is now the richest Dutch person, with an estimated €26 billion. (NOS, NU.nl)
  • Beekse Bergen keeper dies after leopard attack: The keeper, who’d worked at the safari park for nearly 40 years, died of head and neck injuries on Tuesday. (NL Times, DutchNews.nl)
  • Amsterdam’s Transkliniek saved, for now: Alderman Araya Sumter promised extra money to cover part of the clinic’s rent, but it’s unclear whether that’s enough. (Het Parool)
  • Amateurs Excelsior’31 knock ADO out of the cup: Excelsior’31, an amateur club from Rijssen, beat Eredivisie side ADO Den Haag 6–5 on penalties after a 1–1 draw. (NOS, NU.nl)
