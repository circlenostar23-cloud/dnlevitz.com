About

I'm a product manager in Amsterdam. I've spent nine years on data and digital products, and I moved here from the United States, which is why most of these projects are about finding my way around Dutch life.

Each one started as a problem of my own. I wanted to keep up with Dutch news before I could read it, so I built “Dutch Daily Briefing,” a podcast that has put out an episode every morning since September 2026. I wanted to get more out of the Amsterdam Fringe, so I built a theater planner that scores shows against my taste. And my Dutch textbook is written entirely in Dutch, so I set up a tutor that explains each chapter in English. I'm learning slowly and have the A2 certificate to prove it.

I build all of them with AI coding agents. For anything bigger than a quick fix, one model writes a plan and another reviews it and carries it out, as this note explains. I build fixes for small things too, like keeping my saved map lists in order or labeling audio files.

I write these up for other product people who are curious what it takes to build and run something like this end to end. The project pages tell the story of each one, and the notes open up how the parts work, most with the audio or output to try.
