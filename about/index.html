About

I'm a product manager in Amsterdam. I've spent nine years working on data and digital products, and I moved here from the United States, which explains why half of these projects are about finding my way around Dutch life.

I also like to build. The projects on this site started as problems of my own: I couldn't find a podcast that kept me up with Dutch news in English, and I couldn't tell which plays in town I'd be able to follow. I built each fix with AI coding agents and I still use them. I'm sharing them here in case they're interesting or useful to someone else who builds things.

I do this for small things too, like keeping my saved map lists in order or labeling audio files.

I'm learning Dutch, slowly, and have the A2 certificate to prove it. I built a tutor for that too.
