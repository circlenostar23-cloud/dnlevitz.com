Inside the podcast

  • Fact-checkingHow my news podcast checks its own factsWhen the morning's articles leave a question open, a fact-checker looks up the answer before the hosts record.
  • What's OnA “What's On” segment that knows what's newFresh picks from the city's cultural calendar, all fine without Dutch, with the weekend on Thursdays and Fridays.
  • SoundAn audio logo for my podcastLike the "ta-dum" that plays before a Netflix show: a cue marks each story and a theme opens and closes the show, all cut from the Dutch national anthem played backwards.
  • MemoryHow my news podcast remembers the past weekEach morning it reads the past week's episodes, so a story only comes back when something new has happened, as a short update.
  • Show notesChapters, links and live captions for every episodeA chapter per story to skip around, links to each story's articles and live captions while it plays. The same chapters and notes are on every episode's page.
  • VoicesListen to the AI voices that auditioned to host my podcastThe free voices kept getting stuck, so I tested six paid ones side by side for $0.35 and made one the backup.
