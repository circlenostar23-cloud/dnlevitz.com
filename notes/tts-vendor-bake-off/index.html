Listen to the AI voices that auditioned to host my podcast

My podcast's free voices left almost no room for a re-run, so I auditioned six paid ones on the same script for $0.35.

Part of “Dutch Daily Briefing,” my daily English podcast about Dutch news→

The podcast’s hosts, Maya and Sam, are voiced on Gemini’s free text-to-speech tier. One episode uses most of its 10 requests a day, so there’s almost no room to re-run a part that fails. In its first week, it also stalled on several mornings. OpenRouter, a service that gives access to speech models from several companies through one account, let me audition six paid backups on the same script.

Why Gemini kept the job

When I call Gemini directly, it records both hosts in one request, so they sound like they’re talking to each other. Through OpenRouter, each request takes one voice, so the hosts no longer react to each other. Forcing both hosts into one request there failed.

So the free tier still voices the hosts, and Gemini through OpenRouter is the understudy. When a free request fails, only that part of the episode goes through OpenRouter, in the same voices. Free Microsoft voices are the last resort.
