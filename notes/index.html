Inside the podcast

Each note opens up one part of my news podcast, in the order an episode gets made.

  • Fact-checkingHow my news podcast checks its own factsEarly episodes went vague whenever the morning's articles left something out. Now the writer lists what it doesn't know, and a fact-checker looks each question up before the hosts say a word.
  • What's OnA “What's On” segment that knows what's newMy news podcast ends with a few things to do in Amsterdam, picked from the city's calendar. It covers the whole weekend on Thursdays and Fridays, never repeats a recent pick, links every event and can tell a film that opens today from one that's been on for weeks.
  • SoundAn audio logo for my podcastLike the "ta-dum" that plays before a Netflix show, my podcast now has a sound of its own. A cue marks each story and a theme opens and closes the show, all cut from the Wilhelmus played backwards.
  • MemoryHow my news podcast remembers the past weekEach morning my podcast reads its own episodes from the past week, so a story it already told comes back only as a short update when something new has happened.
  • Show notesChapters, links and live captions for every episodeEvery episode of my news podcast now has a chapter for each story, live captions and show notes that link each story's articles, so I can skip ahead, hear a story again or read up on it.
  • VoicesListen to the AI voices that auditioned to host my podcastMy podcast's free voices left almost no room for a re-run, so I auditioned six paid ones on the same script for $0.35.
