How my news podcast remembers the past week

Each morning my podcast reads its own episodes from the past week, so a story it already told comes back only as a short update when something new has happened.

Part of “Dutch Daily Briefing,” my daily English podcast about Dutch news→

The first episodes retold yesterday’s stories as if the listener had never heard them. A cyberattack on Albert Heijn, the biggest Dutch supermarket chain, became the longest the podcast had followed one story: eight episodes over nearly two weeks. The story came back only on days with news and skipped what the listener already knew, so it shrank from over two minutes to 42 seconds.
