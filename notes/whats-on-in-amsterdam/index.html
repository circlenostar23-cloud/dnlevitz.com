A “What's On” segment that knows what's new

My news podcast ends with a few things to do in Amsterdam, picked from the city's calendar. It covers the whole weekend on Thursdays and Fridays, never repeats a recent pick, links every event and can tell a film that opens today from one that's been on for weeks.

Part of “Dutch Daily Briefing,” my daily English podcast about Dutch news→

The news tells me what happened in Amsterdam. The last segment of every episode tells me what I could go and do in it. Code clears what can’t qualify, and two AI passes pick what suits me.
