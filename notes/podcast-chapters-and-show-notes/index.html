Chapters, links and live captions for every episode

Every episode of my news podcast now has a chapter for each story, live captions and show notes that link each story's articles, so I can skip ahead, hear a story again or read up on it.

Part of “Dutch Daily Briefing,” my daily English podcast about Dutch news→

A chapter for every story

A news episode is a dozen stories in a row, and I don’t always want them in order. Each story is a chapter, written into the MP3 with its title, so a podcast app can list the stories and jump straight to one.

Links for every story

The show notes link up to two articles per story, an English one first when there is one. They also hold what the episode had no room for: stories that just missed the cut and more weekend events, in case none of the picks in the “What’s On” segment appeal. When I can’t listen, they’re a quick way to check the day’s news.

Live captions while it plays

Finding the chapters meant timing every line, so each episode also comes with a transcript. Overcast and Pocket Casts show it as live captions, each line under its host’s name.

The same chapters and notes are on every episode’s page.
